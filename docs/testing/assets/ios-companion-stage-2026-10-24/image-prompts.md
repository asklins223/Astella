Not realistic, not watercolor, not painted with brushstrokes, not 3D clay or marshmallow blobs. No little texture or sparkles. A deliberate high-end illustrated background, exceptionally clean and calm, with a subtle sense of the sea and summer light.
No objects, people, characters, animals, clouds, sun, mountains, islands, boats, coral, shells, rocks, plants, buildings, UI, frames, decorative circles, text or watermark. No busy foam, realistic ripples, granular sand, detailed caustics, grain, noise, paper texture, sharp outlines, harsh shadows, neon colors or obvious polygon shapes.
